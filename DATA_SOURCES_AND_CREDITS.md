# UrbanRay AI — Data Sources, Websites, and Development Credits

**Judge-facing source register | Veritas AI 2026 Climate Track**

This document lists external datasets, websites, and software specifically supported by the packaged UrbanRay source code and documentation. External references are **data/tool inputs, not evidence that the outside organizations endorsed or developed this project**. Data used to train the AI were generated by the project's simulator from sourced geometry and weather, **not direct rooftop temperature measurements**.

## A. Primary external datasets and services used

| Source | Exact source / identifier | Role in UrbanRay | Status and limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NYC Open Data — BUILDING (NYC Office of Technology and Innovation)** | [Dataset 5zhs-2jue](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/BUILDING/5zhs-2jue); [JSON API](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/5zhs-2jue.json); [official footprint metadata](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/master/Metadata/Metadata_BuildingFootprints.md) | Source of building footprint geometries and roof-height fields used for the NYC block | The local, simplified 88-building geometry is in `data/nyc_88_source.geojson`. This subset is **not** a current official citywide dataset, and its simplified geometry does not capture all rooftop details. |
| **Open-Meteo — Historical Weather API** | [Official documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api); [archive endpoint](https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive); [data terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) | Historical modeled/gridded weather, including temperature, wind, cloud cover, direct normal irradiance and diffuse radiation | Accessed by `fetch_weather_range.py` and `src/urbanray.py`. Reanalysis/model estimates, **not roof-station measurements**. All raw inputs from previous runs are **not packaged**. Open-Meteo data is attributed under CC BY 4.0; see license link below. |
| **USGS Landsat 8 Collection 2 Level-2 (via Google Earth Engine)** | [Earth Engine collection `LANDSAT/LC08/C02/T1_L2`](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/LANDSAT_LC08_C02_T1_L2); [USGS surface-temperature guide](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-temperature) | Historical land-surface-temperature proxy comparison and scene/date screening | Used in `earth_engine/UrbanRay_Find_Matching_Landsat_Dates.js`; surface temperature is **not an individual roof thermometer**, nor validation of retrofit outcomes. |
| **USGS Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level-2 (via Google Earth Engine)** | [Earth Engine collection `LANDSAT/LC09/C02/T1_L2`](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/LANDSAT_LC09_C02_T1_L2) | Additional satellite scene screening and historical thermal reference | Same observational limits as Landsat 8; these inputs do not train the simulation-derived AI directly. |
| **Google Earth Engine Code Editor and data catalog** | [Code Editor](https://code.earthengine.google.com/); [dataset catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets) | Execution environment for the Landsat retrieval/export script | Platform facilitating access to USGS data; **Google Earth Engine is not the creator of the Landsat observations**. |

**Appropriate data acknowledgement:** "Building geometry: NYC Open Data / NYC Office of Technology and Innovation. Gridded historical weather: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Satellite surface-temperature products: USGS Landsat 8–9 Collection 2 Level-2, accessed through Google Earth Engine."

## B. Local packaged datasets, generated outputs, and evidence

| Local path | Type and origin | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| `data/nyc_88_source.geojson` | Local geometry subset derived from NYC municipal building records | Geometry for the 88-building simulation (verify the saved provenance/audit before claiming a particular extraction date) |
| `urbanray_all_scene_inventory_2025_2026.csv` | Project-generated Landsat scene inventory | Satellite scene selection and audit |
| `urbanray_qualified_matching_dates_2025_2026.csv` | Project-generated satellite date selection | Historical scene/date matching |
| `urbanray_qualified_landsat_pixels_2025_2026.csv` | Project-exported / processed Landsat grid-cell data | Exploratory observational comparison |
| `outputs/real_world_upgrade/*_SIMULATED.csv` | Project-generated physics/AI simulator benchmarks | Simulator-relative decision evaluation **only** |
| `outputs/geometry_ai_upgrade/` and `outputs/ai_upgrade/` | Project-generated trained model artifacts and benchmark files | Inference and evaluation against simulation labels |
| `outputs/decision_validation_gate/report.md` | Project-generated evidence audit | Clarifies which decision claims are supported |
| `VALIDATION_2025_SUMMARY.json` | Transcription of five reported per-date validation summaries | **Not** an independently reproduced five-date raw-observation experiment in this ZIP |

**Incomplete materials:** The curated ZIP does not include the approximately 131 MB historical face-hour training table, all raw historical weather responses, or the full per-date raw satellite matching logs. Do not call the ZIP fully self-contained for original-model retraining or complete satellite replication. The available code can re-fetch some data, but external services, changing source versions, and missing original artifacts may prevent exact reproduction.

## F. Source-to-code traceability

- `src/urbanray.py` → NYC Open Data JSON API; Open-Meteo archive API.
- `download_nyc_context.py` → documented optional municipal geometry API fetch.
- `fetch_weather_range.py` → Open-Meteo historical hourly data and response hash metadata.
- `earth_engine/UrbanRay_Find_Matching_Landsat_Dates.js` → two USGS Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2 Earth Engine collections.
- `validate_landsat_pixels.py` and `validate_unified_landsat.py` → observational grid-cell matching and explicitly exploratory comparisons.
- `train_geometry_ai.py`, `train_reflection_residual.py`, `train_surrogate.py` → model training on simulated labels and saved training features.
- `requirements.txt` → exact Python package requirements.

### Official online verification notes

Official dataset/API/catalog pages were checked while preparing this register. An online source page may have a newer modification date than the project's historical data extraction.
