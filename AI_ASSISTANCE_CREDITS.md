# AI Assistance and Development Credits

**Project:** UrbanRay AI — Urban Radiation and Reflection Decision Engine  
**Competition:** Veritas AI International High School Hackathon 2026  
**Assistant:** OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-6) — https://chatgpt.com/

## Scope of assistance

The team used ChatGPT as a research, software development, and documentation assistant. Its contributions included:

1. **Coding and debugging:** Helping write, update, review, and troubleshoot Python source, model and physics-integration code, dashboard-related code, validation scripts, decision-audit logic, and automated tests. The team reviewed and directed which changes were incorporated.
2. **Dataset research and citations:** Helping locate and verify official source websites for NYC building geometry, Open-Meteo historical weather, USGS Landsat products, and Google Earth Engine; organizing links, licensing notes, and dataset-to-code traceability. See `DATA_SOURCES_AND_CREDITS.md` for the actual sources and their roles.
3. **GitHub preparation:** Helping organize the repository layout, draft README and judge-facing documents, package ZIP files, and explain methods for uploading or updating project files on GitHub. This does **not** claim ChatGPT itself pushed commits or operated the team's GitHub account.
4. **Questions and troubleshooting:** Discussing technical and scientific uncertainties, model comparisons, implementation errors, physical assumptions, evaluation designs, and competition submission requirements.
5. **Evidence and presentation:** Helping construct conservative test and benchmark disclosures, run available local checks, prepare demonstration notes, and document what simulator outputs and observational comparisons do—and do not—establish.

## Responsibility and accuracy

Human team members decided the project goals and final submission content, directed development, evaluated suggested changes, and remain responsible for verifying accuracy, authorship, and compliance with competition rules. AI assistance is not an independent external validation. Model tests against physics-simulated labels are distinguished from real-world measured outcomes, and no measured rooftop cooling or independently confirmed decision benefit is claimed.

The team reports that project development occurred during **November 14–15, 2026**. Supporting timestamps and original records, rather than this statement, provide any independent evidence of development chronology.
